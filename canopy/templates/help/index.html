How to move a plant through its life

Every transition, and where to click for it. Groups are containers — a plant on its own flips, schedules and harvests exactly like one in a batch.

The one rule

The schedule belongs to the plant. A plant's flip date is the last time it entered flower, and its harvest is that plus its flower length. A group has no dates of its own — it shows the span of whatever is inside it.

So moving one plant and moving a whole group do the same thing. A plant with no group still gets a flip date, a projected harvest and a bar on the timeline.

Starting something

From seed
Plants → Add plant. Set status {{ badge('seedling') }} and type the strain — a new pack does not need adding to inventory first. Leave Location blank and it goes where seedlings live ({{ VEG }}).
Seed counts in Inventory are yours to adjust with − / +; nothing changes them for you.
A clone from outside
Plants → Add plant, status {{ badge('clone') }}, location {{ CLONE }}. Type the strain name — if it is not in inventory yet it gets added as you save.
A cutting off your own plant
Plant page → Take n cuttings into … Creates them in one go, numbered from the mother ({{ 'Mango #7 c1'|safe }}, c2, …) and continuing where a previous batch left off. Each is linked back, so the cutting shows Taken from and the mother shows Cuttings.

Ending something

Kill a plant
Plant page → Mark as killed, with a date and a reason. Works at any stage.
Killed plants stay on the record — out of counts and capacity, but keeping their reason so the survival and loss reports mean something.
Harvest the whole group
Group page → Harvest → Record a harvest, with a date and anything worth remembering. Every plant it covers becomes {{ badge('harvested') }} with an end date, and the group moves to {{ badge('drying') }} once nothing is left in flower. Setting the group's status to {{ badge('drying') }} or {{ badge('done') }} by hand does the same to its plants.
Cut a single plant
Plant page → Set status → {{ badge('harvested') }}, or Group page → Harvest → Record a harvest for just that plant. The rest of the group keeps flowering; when the last one is cut the group moves to {{ badge('drying') }} on its own.
{{ badge('harvested') }} is the plant's word for what the group calls {{ badge('drying') }} — a plant that has been cut and hung. There is no separate drying status for a plant.

No location set
A plant with no explicit space sits in the first space matching its stage. Setting one pins it there.

Good to know

Life so far
Every status change is logged, so each plant page shows a strip of how long it spent as a cutting, in veg and in flower.
Lineage
Two kinds. A strain's lineage is its genetics, typed on the strain or on the add-plant form. A plant's parent is the actual plant it was cut from, which walks back up the line.
Journal
Notes only. Log a whole tent from the dashboard, with a photo if you want one. Nothing in the app reads entries back or derives anything from them.
Photos
Attach one to any journal entry — the Photo button on the daily log, or the full entry form. They live in instance/uploads, outside the database and outside git, so a JSON backup carries the filename but not the picture.
Capacity
An estimate from tent dimensions and per-plant footprints, shown as a label on Spaces — never as an alert. It does not know your pot sizes, so a tray of cuttings can read as needing far more room than it does.
Empty groups
A group with no plants cannot be scheduled — there is nothing to give a date to. Add plants first.
