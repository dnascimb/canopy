01The problem

The timeline drew flower windows only. Everything before the flip was missing: rooting cuttings, seedlings, and plants in veg waiting for a slot.

The veg tent holds 32 and is carrying 35. The flower room it feeds is at 30 of 36. The timeline covered the room with space and ignored the one without.

02Design

One toggle, labelled Full cycle. Off is the default and is the chart that already existed. On adds the pre-flower span to each bar and brings in units that have not flipped. The toggle only appears when there is pre-flower data.

An earlier version had three buttons — Flower, Veg, All. One toggle does the same job with less on screen.

03The bar is pre-flower, not veg

A strict veg bar would read each plant's first vegetative event. Almost nothing has one.

Pre-flower bars that draw

The span runs from the first lifecycle event that is not the flip, falling back to started_on, up to the flip — or up to today if it has not flipped. It means "alive, not yet flowering". It gets more accurate as more transitions are logged.

04The axis

Server-side bounds come from flower spans only. Pre-flower spans reach months further back, so including them would shrink every flower bar in the default view. With the toggle off the chart uses the server's bounds and is unchanged. With it on the renderer widens the axis itself. There is a test for that.

05Two things this exposed

A bug

The first render drew one-pixel bars against six units. A started_on entered after the fact can be later than the flip it precedes: Jack Herer was recorded as starting 7 September and flipping 4 September, a span of minus three days.

pre_flower_start() now returns nothing when the earliest known date is not earlier than the flip. 25 candidates became 19 real bars.

Seeded history

Six older groups show a pre-flower span of exactly 56 days. That is demo data — the seeder sets started_on to a fixed offset from the flip. Runs logged since the lifecycle log was added vary. This is the first screen that shows which history was measured.

06Limits

  • Pre-flower is one span. Clone, seedling and vegetative are not separated.
  • The toggle is not remembered. Every load starts off.
  • Veg load is drawn, not projected. No warning that the veg tent will be over its count in two weeks.
  • Nine units have no pre-flower date and draw nothing. Older plants with no events and no start date.
